Meet the Developers

The student developers behind Vellore Discover and its mission to make exploring Vellore more interactive, informative and engaging.

ABOUT THE DEVELOPERS

Built by Students, Inspired by Vellore

✨

Vellore Discover was developed by three student developers — Vansh, Aniket Verma, and Chandru. All three are currently pursuing their studies at VIT Vellore and have contributed to the design and development of this multimedia-based local discovery platform.

Together, we created Vellore Discover to provide an interactive and engaging way for people to explore the heritage, culture, nature, landmarks, and lesser-known destinations of Vellore.
